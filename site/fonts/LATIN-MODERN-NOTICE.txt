The six .woff2 files here are derived from the OpenType files of the same
names in the original distribution:

  lmroman10-regular  lmroman10-italic  lmroman10-bold
  lmroman17-regular  lmmono10-regular  lmmonolt10-bold

The only change is the container: each font's tables were repacked, byte
for byte, as WOFF2 for use on the web. No glyph, metric or name was
changed. These files are not supported by the original authors.

The unmodified fonts are at https://ctan.org/pkg/lm, in
fonts/lm/fonts/opentype/public/lm/.
