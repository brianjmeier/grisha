The .woff2 files in this folder are cut-down copies of two font families,
both released under the GUST Font License, which is in
GUST-FONT-LICENSE.txt in this folder.

New Computer Modern, version 8.1.1, by Antonis Tsolomitis
  newcm10-book-latin        from NewCM10-Book.otf
  newcm10-bookitalic-latin  from NewCM10-BookItalic.otf
  newcm10-bold-latin        from NewCM10-Bold.otf
  newcmmono10-book-latin    from NewCMMono10-Book.otf
  newcmmono10-bold-latin    from NewCMMono10-Bold.otf
  The unmodified fonts are at https://ctan.org/pkg/newcomputermodern

Latin Modern, version 2.004, by B. Jackowski and J.M. Nowacki (on behalf
of TeX Users Groups)
  lmroman17-regular-latin   from lmroman17-regular.otf
  The unmodified fonts are at https://ctan.org/pkg/lm

Designer of the Computer Modern family: Donald E. Knuth.

These files are modified: each keeps only the Latin letters, digits and
punctuation this website uses, and is repacked as WOFF2. Nothing else was
changed. They are not supported by the original authors, and they are not
a replacement for the original fonts.
